Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about blood donation

Eligibility

Who can donate blood?

Generally, healthy adults aged 18-65 weighing at least 50kg can donate.

How often can I donate?

Every 56 days (8 weeks) for whole blood donation.

Can I donate if I have a cold?

No, you should be feeling healthy on the day of donation.

What medications disqualify me?

Some medications may require waiting periods. Check with our staff.

The Donation Process

How long does donation take?

The entire process takes about 1 hour, with actual donation 8-10 minutes.

Is donating blood safe?

Yes, sterile equipment is used once and discarded.

Will it hurt?

You may feel a quick pinch, but most donors feel fine.

How much blood is taken?

About 1 pint (450-500ml), which your body quickly replaces.

After Donation

What should I eat after donating?

Iron-rich foods and plenty of fluids.

Can I exercise after donating?

Avoid strenuous exercise for 24 hours.

When will I know my blood type?

You'll receive notification after your donation is tested.

How soon can I donate again?

56 days for whole blood, sooner for platelets.
