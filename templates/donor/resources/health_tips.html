Health Tips for Donors

Stay healthy and ready to donate with tips tailored for Kenya

Before Your Donation
Drink Plenty of Water

Drink at least 8 glasses of water the day before and morning of donation. In Kenyan weather, stay extra hydrated!

Eat Iron-Rich Foods

Include Kenyan foods like Sukuma wiki, Omena, beef, beans, and ndengu in your meals 2-3 days before donation.

Get Good Sleep

Aim for 7-8 hours of sleep the night before your donation to feel refreshed.

Avoid Fatty Foods

Avoid heavy, fatty meals (like nyama choma with lots of fat) 4 hours before donation.

During Your Donation
Relax and Breathe

Take deep breaths and relax. The actual donation takes only 8-10 minutes.

Communicate with Staff

Tell the phlebotomist if you feel dizzy, nauseous, or uncomfortable.

Keep Arm Still

Keep your arm still and relaxed during the donation process.

After Your Donation
Rest for 10-15 Minutes

Stay at the donation center and enjoy the refreshments provided.

Drink Extra Fluids

Drink plenty of water, juice, or soft drinks for the next 24-48 hours.

Avoid Heavy Exercise

Skip strenuous activities and heavy lifting for 24 hours.

Eat Iron-Rich Foods

Continue eating iron-rich Kenyan foods for the next few weeks.

Kenya-Specific Tips
Malaria Prevention

If you've had malaria, wait 6 months after recovery and treatment before donating.

Travel to Malaria Areas

If you've traveled to malaria-endemic areas (Coast, Western Kenya), wait 6 months after return.

Sickle Cell Trait

Donors with sickle cell trait can donate blood. This is common in Kenya.

Hot Weather Precautions

In hot Kenyan weather, increase water intake and avoid donating during the hottest part of the day.
